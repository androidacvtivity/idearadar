## Description

Turn scattered thoughts into clear opportunities with IdeaRadar.

IdeaRadar helps you capture, evaluate, research, and review ideas in one focused workspace. Record an idea in seconds, describe the problem and target users, assess its potential with a simple score, and support your decisions with notes and sources.

Key features:

- Capture and edit ideas quickly
- Evaluate ideas with a clear, explainable score
- Add research notes and supporting sources
- Search, filter, sort, archive, and restore ideas
- Schedule the next review for promising opportunities
- Keep your work private with offline storage

IdeaRadar requires no account. Your ideas remain stored locally on your device.
