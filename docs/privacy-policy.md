# IdeaRadar Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 16, 2026

IdeaRadar is an offline-first application for capturing, evaluating, and tracking ideas.

## Data collection

IdeaRadar does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

## Data stored on the device

Ideas, evaluations, notes, sources, and review dates entered in IdeaRadar are stored locally on the user's device. This information is not sent to the developer or to third parties.

Deleting IdeaRadar from the device removes its locally stored application data, subject to the device operating system's backup and restore behavior.

## Third-party services

IdeaRadar does not use third-party services that collect user data.

## Children's privacy

IdeaRadar is a general productivity application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if IdeaRadar's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the [IdeaRadar support page](https://github.com/androidacvtivity/idearadar/blob/main/docs/support.md) or open a [GitHub support request](https://github.com/androidacvtivity/idearadar/issues).
